Preparation

1

Prep & Pre-bake

Preheat oven to 200°C. Press your leftover pastry or pie crust into a pan. Blind bake for 8 minutes until just firm.

2

The Leftover Mix

Whisk 4 eggs with 200ml cream. Fold in chopped leftover bacon, ham, or sautéed greens. Season with a pinch of nutmeg.

3

Bake to Golden

Pour mixture into the crust. Sprinkle with leftover cheese. Bake for 15-18 minutes until set and beautifully golden.

lightbulb

Pro-tip: If you have leftover roast onions, caramelize them slightly and add for extra depth of flavor.
